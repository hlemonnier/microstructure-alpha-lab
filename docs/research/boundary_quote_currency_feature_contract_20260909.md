# Alternative spot-market features: currency and observation contract

The prototype retains the existing spot trade windows and learner-independent feature definitions. It supplements them with an explicitly observed conversion rate, its age and availability, and a converted-basis availability indicator. It does not fit a model or establish a predictive improvement.

The conversion rate has units of target quote currency per native quote currency. For BTC/TUSD observations and the BTC/USDT target, the proposed conversion source is TUSD/USDT spot aggregate trades. For ETH/BTC observations and the ETH/USDT target, it is BTC/USDT spot aggregate trades, already available locally and represented in the existing combined baseline. A later ablation must retain the conversion observations in the matched control to distinguish native-market information from the conversion source itself.

At decision time d, a conversion observation with publisher timestamp u is eligible only when u plus the declared conversion delay is strictly less than d. The newest eligible observation must also satisfy the declared maximum age. Native spot trades retain their separate strict 100ms or 500ms information delay. These are publisher-time assumptions; they do not certify real feed arrival times. Integer clock validation rejects precision loss, overflow and regressions, including unsigned subtraction that could conceal a decreasing clock.

The target bid and ask are divided by the eligible conversion rate, putting the reference price in native quote units. Native trade quantities remain in their original base asset, which matches the target's base asset. Price-basis features therefore compare compatible units. A native-quote price multiplier c, paired with a conversion-rate multiplier 1/c, preserves converted price bases and native returns. The explicit log conversion-rate coordinate changes by minus log(c), as its definition requires.

Native spot return fields remain returns in native quote currency. Window VWAP bases value the historical native-quote VWAP at the current eligible conversion rate. They do not claim to be averages of historically converted execution prices, executable quotes or realized trading returns.

When conversion coverage is absent or stale, native returns and trade-flow features remain available. Converted-basis fields are masked. Such rows are excluded from the running basis mean before it is updated; merely masking the displayed value would leave an invalid conversion in the state and contaminate later forecasts. All original decision rows remain present. Historical spot availability means that a prior trade exists, with its age explicit; it is not a guarantee of a fresh spot quote.

Seven focused tests pass across the new conversion wrapper and the existing spot-feature implementation. They cover conversion direction, equality at the information cutoff, future-prefix independence, stale-conversion recovery, missing conversion history, native-quote unit changes, exact identity-conversion replay, ignored target columns, and invalid clock/delay representations. No new market accuracy was scored during this work. The running counted-depth experiment and reserved independent dates remain unchanged.
