# Counted-depth predictive experiment, fixed before fitting

The hypothesis is that an additional exchange's queue composition improves the existing five-second, three-class forecast. The primary metric remains mean balanced accuracy across the same two assets and three exposed development dates. Natural accuracy and natural log loss remain secondary diagnostics. No target, prediction horizon, row universe or independent-confirmation requirement changes.

The source window is May 29–June 11, 2023, for BTC and ETH. Assessment dates are June 3, 7 and 11. Each fold trains on d-5 through d-2 with the original calendar stride of four seconds, selects a neural checkpoint using the previous day's noon window, and evaluates all 7,070 original noon decisions per asset. Historical outcome release times must precede the next partition. All 28 source caches must complete, and all three fold preparations must pass, before the first new fit.

The data comparison has six variants: quantity-only versus quantity-plus-count at 25 levels/100ms, 100 levels/100ms, and 100 levels/500ms. Each pair uses the same source, masks and original rows. Counts add log order totals, count imbalance, relative mean order sizes, singleton-price-level fractions, concentration across price levels and sampled count-pressure changes. Quantity features are an exact subset of the corresponding count-inclusive cache. Shape normalizations stay within the OKX book; quantities are not divided by unconverted Binance base-currency quantities.

The quantity-only control also receives log absolute published quantity at each retained depth total. Omitting that scale would weaken the control and could make count totals appear valuable merely as a proxy for missing total depth. Count-inclusive models additionally receive log mean published quantity per order using those identical totals. These supplementary fields are derived from the verified raw sidecars during model input preparation, while the existing shape caches remain unchanged. An asset indicator remains explicit and preprocessing is fitted only on training data. No conversion of the provider's historical quantity units into coins or notional is asserted. Unlike the separate shape features, these absolute-scale fields have an explicit logarithmic response to a unit multiplier.

Two existing representations are tested. The combined representation retains all 399 original Binance/spot/Bybit columns and tests all six variants. The observation representation retains the original 220 Binance observation columns and tests the four top-100 variants. Including the latter permits a direct follow-up against the established twenty-date observation benchmark without relying on unmeasured transfer of the larger source union.

Each variant/representation uses the unchanged class-balanced HGB and neural training procedures. HGB has 200 iterations, learning rate 0.05, seven leaves, minimum leaf size 100, L2 regularization 10, no early stopping and seed 20260907. A matched 31-leaf capacity comparison is added only for the combined top-100/100ms quantity/count pair. Neural models retain the train-only quantile transform, 64-unit hidden layers, 12 epochs, AdamW learning rate 0.001, weight decay 0.01, batch size 1,024, gradient clipping at 5 and the same seed. The previous-noon balanced-accuracy maximum selects the checkpoint; natural log loss breaks ties and the earlier epoch wins an exact tie.

This produces **22 fits per date, 66 total**. Fixed 50:50 blends combine each pair's new tree and neural models, each new tree with its representation's old neural model, and each new neural model with the retained deep-500ms tree. The two 31-leaf models also receive the fixed old-neural blend. There are 32 new blends, eight retained controls and **744 reporting panels** across two decision rules, two assets and three dates. No blend weight is optimized on assessment outcomes.

The primary decision rule uses each procedure's natural probabilities divided by its causal forecast-prior estimate, with the existing 3,600-second half-life and identical initial prior state. New registered-rule diagnostics use historical training priors. Retained legacy registered rules remain method-specific. Balanced training probabilities are converted back to natural probabilities using their training asset/class priors before blending or log-loss evaluation.

The retained controls include the original reference, observation tree/neural/blend, combined tree/neural, deep-500ms tree and strongest retained complete instantaneous-feature blend. Original combined tree and neural checkpoint forecasts, original target labels, clocks and training priors must reproduce exactly during preparation. The separate expert-feedback leader is compared in the final analysis from its already frozen results.

The shared input cache preserves original columns exactly and stores each depth/delay group once. Worker column selection removes only the explicitly excluded count fields and, for the observation representation, the original auxiliary venue fields. Training workers receive training/validation labels and query features; assessment-label paths are absent from their query metadata. New probabilities are produced and checkpoint parity is checked before any new assessment score is computed. All three dates finish before the reporting stage.

Each model runs in its own CPU process with two numerical threads. The planned worker RSS limit is 8GiB, sampled every 0.2 seconds, and its wall limit is 900 seconds. Saved-checkpoint probabilities and training priors must be exact. Changing later rows of an equal-sized query block must have zero effect on earlier forecasts; full/shorter/individual query comparisons must stay within an absolute probability tolerance of 2e-6. These checks do not establish live execution latency.

Every fixed candidate is reported, including losses. Count-specific claims require the matched count-minus-quantity comparison; a gain from quantity alone is additional venue information. A candidate needs at least a 0.5-percentage-point mean gain over the retained complete procedure, gains on both assets, and a natural log-loss ratio no worse than 1.01 to justify the next broader data expansion from this screen. This development screening rule does not replace the existing substantial-gain gate: at least five points over the original registered reference, gains on both assets, the frozen uncertainty criterion and at least twenty independent dates. The reserved June 13–July 2 sources remain unopened for assessment, and this screen consumes no confirmation round.
