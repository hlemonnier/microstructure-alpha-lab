# Performance research path

The objective is higher out-of-sample executable net payoff under the same capital, observed liquidity and trading constraints. This document defines hypotheses and decision branches; it reports no measured performance. The active pilot is specified in [the amended protocol](performance_pilot_20260907_window_revision.json). Its original midnight-window protocol remains preserved; the amendment records a source-coverage reason established before fitting or inspecting outcomes.

The first experiment separates two mechanisms. `ridge_state` versus `ridge_default` measures the incremental value of decision-time spread, depth, activity, quote age and OFI interactions. `hgb_state` versus `ridge_state` measures whether a fixed shallow nonlinear learner uses that same information more effectively. Every learner predicts the same long and short executable gross returns. Validation selects the threshold, including an always-flat control; one persistent account supplies each session's economic result.

This ordering has a research basis. Cont, Kukanov and Stoikov associate short-interval equity price changes with order-flow imbalance and find impact sensitivity related inversely to depth. This motivates flow/depth conditioning; their contemporaneous impact relationship does not itself prove forward predictability. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402)

Kolm, Turiel and Westray find stationary order-flow inputs useful for forecasting multiple horizons, including with comparatively simple networks. Their stock-market findings motivate representation experiments here, without establishing transfer to crypto or profitability after this pilot's execution costs. [Primary paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3900141)

| A repeatable representation gain survives broader data and costs, but the shallow learner still leaves structured forecast residuals | Compare one small causal sequence learner against the strongest fixed tabular baseline using the same inputs, targets and policy. | Architecture becomes a test of incremental temporal information. Require multiple independent date blocks, seed replication and checkpoint parity; reject a gain restricted to one seed, asset or regime. True multi-level order-flow inputs require validated L2 data. |

The event-time branch is motivated by evidence that queue imbalance can help forecast the next price movement, with different behavior across tick-size regimes. The proposed crypto test must separately establish its economic horizon and execution value. [Gould and Bonart](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03492)

The holding-rule branch is motivated by models in which transaction costs induce thresholds or regions where retaining the existing position is preferable to trading. That result depends on assumptions about signal dynamics, risk and costs; the repository must estimate its own tradeoff in replay. [de Lataillade, Deremble, Potters and Bouchaud](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.5957)

For every branch, register the complete candidate family, selection metric, cost scenarios, primary latency, stress latency, data partitions and kill criteria before opening new evaluation outcomes. Compare paired same-date net PnL against both the original ridge and flat; also report turnover, drawdown, gross forecast error, observed fill quantities, cost frontier, latency sensitivity and residual inventory. A cost frontier obtained by selecting a different validation policy at each fee is distinct from repricing one unchanged policy.

Three distinct OOS dates support descriptive screening only. May 24–26 are reserved and excluded from this pilot; that reservation is not a completed formal holdout protocol. Before using them, freeze the candidate or selection procedure, dataset identities, execution contract and immutable evaluation manifest. If those dates are used to compare or tune new variants, they become development evidence and a later untouched range is required for final evaluation. Never tune on this pilot's OOS outcomes and report the same observations again as fresh evidence.
