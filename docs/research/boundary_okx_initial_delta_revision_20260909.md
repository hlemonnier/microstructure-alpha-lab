This was a source-initialization assumption failure, not a predictive result.

The old reader, preparation script, tests and protocol were copied into `data/research/boundary_okx_initial_delta_revision_20260909` with their original hashes.

A dedicated regression verifies missing observations before initialization, strict exclusion at the first snapshot's timestamp, and invariance when a prefix ends before that snapshot.
